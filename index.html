I’m sorry, Iris.

There’s something I want you to know, from my heart.

Iris,

I’m not really sure how to start this, so I’ll just say it plainly: I’m sorry.

I’m sorry for the times I didn’t read the room. There were moments when you needed space, or quiet, or just a little patience, and I didn’t notice. I was so focused on what I was feeling that I missed what you were going through.

I also know I sometimes let my feelings get ahead of me. When that happens, it can feel like you have to carry them for me, on top of everything you’re already carrying. That isn’t fair to you, and I don’t want you to ever feel like that’s your job.

🤎

I’ve been thinking about what loving you really means. Saying “I love you” is the easy part. The harder part is understanding you, respecting your space, and paying attention to what you actually need, even when it’s not what I expected.

I don’t want to keep apologizing for the same things without really changing. That would just be words, and you deserve more than that. I’m still learning, and I know I’ll probably still get things wrong sometimes. But I want you to be able to see the effort in what I do, not just hear it from me.

I love you, and I appreciate you, mahal ko. I want us to feel peaceful and safe with each other. Both of us.

And please, don’t feel like you have to answer, reassure me, or forgive me right away just because I made this. Take all the time you need.

What I want to do better

1To understand you better

I’ll try to be more mindful of the situation and your feelings instead of focusing only on what I’m feeling in the moment.

2To respect your space

I want you to have room to rest, focus on your responsibilities, and be yourself without feeling pressured to give me attention all the time.

3To show it through my actions

I don’t want my apologies to be just words. I want to learn from my mistakes and become more thoughtful, patient, and considerate.

One thing I hope you know

I love you on the easy days, pero lalo na on the days when we’re still figuring each other out.

Not understanding each other sometimes doesn’t change that. It just reminds me to slow down, listen more, and be gentler. Your feelings matter to me, Iris.

I’m sorry for the times I didn’t get things right. I love you, and I want to keep learning how to love you better. You don’t have to say anything right now. Take your time, mahal ko. I’ll do my best to let my actions speak too. 🤎

Always choosing to learn, grow, and love you better.
