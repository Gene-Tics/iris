a letter from me

dear iris,

it feels so special to finally celebrate our first monthsary as an official couple. parang kahapon lang, kinakabahan pa akong magtanong, and now i get to call you my girlfriend. thank you for choosing me. i don’t take that lightly.

i’m grateful for you, for your presence, for your personality, for all the little things that make you so uniquely you. the way you are is enough for me. i’m not looking for a perfect girlfriend. i’m just happy it’s you.

i also want you to know that i don’t expect all of your time and energy. you have your own life, your own pace, your own days when you need space or quiet. that’s okay. you don’t have to earn my love by being available all the time. i want to love you in a way that feels safe, respectful, and light, a love where you’re free to just be yourself.

i’m sorry for the times i made mistakes, overthought things, or unintentionally made you feel overwhelmed. hindi ko iyon sinasadya, pero totoo na may epekto iyon sa’yo, and i’m sorry. i’m learning, and i’m trying to be better, little by little.

because for me, love isn’t only sweet words. it’s learning, improving, respecting your boundaries, and showing up through my actions, especially on ordinary days.

thank you for this first month. i’m really happy.

— eugene

two small poems

the girl i get to call mine

there’s a quiet kind of beauty
in the way you simply are,
not loud, not hurried,
but steady, like a soft light
left on for someone coming home.

i didn’t know that being chosen
could feel this gentle,
that a single “yes” from you
could make the whole day
feel kinder than it was.

ordinary things became small gifts:
a message, a laugh,
a silence we don’t have to fill.
you turn the plain hours golden
without even trying.

and when i think of luck,
i don’t think of stars or wishes.
i think of you,
the girl i get to call mine,
and i say thank you, quietly, again.

my favorite kind of forever

i don’t need a grand forever,
only this one:
a tuesday, a tired smile,
and the choice to stay kind
when it would be easier not to.

let’s grow slowly,
like things that last do,
with patience for each other’s weather,
and room to be unfinished
and still be loved.

let love be a habit we keep,
small and honest and every day,
listening more than we defend,
trying again without keeping score.

that’s my favorite kind of forever,
the kind we build together,
one ordinary day at a time.
